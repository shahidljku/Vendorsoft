About Us

About VendorPro

VendorPro is a billing and route management application purpose-built for newspaper vendors, distributors, and subscription-based delivery agencies across India.

Our Mission

Simplify Operations for Newspaper Vendors

Thousands of newspaper vendors across India manage their daily operations — customer records, monthly billing, payment collection, and delivery routes — using pen-and-paper ledgers or basic notebooks. This manual process is time-consuming, error-prone, and difficult to scale.

VendorPro was created to give these vendors a simple, affordable mobile tool that automates their core business operations without requiring internet access, cloud storage, or technical expertise. The app runs entirely on the vendor's Android phone and stores all data locally.

What We Stand For

Our Core Principles

📴 Offline First

Your business data stays on your phone. VendorPro's core engine works without internet — billing and printing function fully offline. Internet is used securely only for backups and WhatsApp dispatch.

🔒 Data Ownership

We believe your business data belongs to you. Customer records, bills, and payment information are never uploaded to any cloud server.

💰 Affordable

VendorPro is priced for Indian vendors running small to medium agencies. A 30-day free trial lets you evaluate the product before purchasing.

🛠️ Purpose-Built

VendorPro is not a generic billing app. Every feature is designed specifically for newspaper and magazine distribution workflows.

Product

What VendorPro Does

VendorPro is a mobile application for Android that helps newspaper vendors:

  • Manage customer records with newspaper selections, delivery addresses, and monthly rates
  • Generate and calculate monthly bills automatically
  • Send bills directly to customers via WhatsApp
  • Print thermal receipts on 58mm/80mm Bluetooth printers for morning delivery
  • Track customer payments and outstanding balances
  • Manage delivery routes and collection assignments
  • Generate PDF reports for agency records
  • Back up and restore data from local storage

The application is designed to be simple enough for vendors who may not be technically experienced, while being robust enough to handle agencies with thousands of customers.
