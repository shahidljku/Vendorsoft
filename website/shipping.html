Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: July 2026

VendorPro is a digital software product. This Shipping and Delivery Policy explains how our product and subscription services are delivered to you.

1. Digital Product

VendorPro is a mobile application (Android APK) that is downloaded and installed directly on your device. There are no physical goods shipped as part of any VendorPro purchase.

2. App Download

The VendorPro application can be downloaded from the following sources:

  • Directly from this website (APK download link, when available)

Downloading and installing the App requires an Android device with a working internet connection.

3. Subscription Activation

After a successful payment via Razorpay, your subscription is activated automatically within the App. Activation typically occurs within seconds of payment confirmation. No manual action is required beyond completing the payment.

If your subscription does not activate within a few minutes after payment, you can use the "Check Payment Status" option within the App, or contact our support team for assistance.

4. No Physical Shipping

Since VendorPro is entirely a digital product, there are no shipping charges, delivery timelines, or physical logistics involved. All transactions are for digital subscription licenses delivered electronically.

5. Service Availability

VendorPro's core features (billing, printing, WhatsApp dispatch) work offline without internet. However, the following operations require an active internet connection:

  • Initial app registration
  • Subscription purchase and activation
  • Device transfer
  • License status verification
