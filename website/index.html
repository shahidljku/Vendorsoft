Features

Everything You Need to Run Your Agency

Built specifically for newspaper vendors, distributors, and subscription-based agencies across India.

Customer Management

Manage thousands of customers with details like newspaper selection, delivery routes, and monthly rates — stored securely on your device using a fast local SQLite database.

WhatsApp Bill Dispatch

Automatically send monthly bills to customers via WhatsApp in safe batched intervals. No manual messaging needed — just tap and let VendorPro handle the rest.

Bluetooth Thermal Printing

Reports & Payment Tracking

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

VendorPro is a mobile billing and route management application built for Indian newspaper vendors, distributors, and subscription-based agencies. It helps you manage customers, generate monthly bills, send them via WhatsApp, and print thermal receipts — all from your Android phone.

VendorPro's core billing engine operates offline. Your business data — customer lists, bills, payment records — is stored locally on your phone. You can generate bills and print thermal receipts without any internet connection. However, sending WhatsApp messages, cloud backups, and subscription verification require an active internet connection.

Your business data is stored 100% locally on your phone in a fast local SQLite database. No customer data, bills, or payment records are ever uploaded to any cloud server. You maintain complete control and ownership of your data.

When you first install VendorPro, you get a full 30-day trial with access to core features. During the trial, you can manage unlimited customers. No credit card or payment is required. After the trial, you can choose a monthly or yearly plan to continue with full access.

Your data is never deleted. Since all data is stored locally on your device, it remains safe even after the trial expires. You simply won't be able to access certain features until you activate a paid plan. Once activated, all your data is immediately available again.

Yes. VendorPro includes a built-in device transfer feature. Install the app on your new phone and request a transfer. Your old phone will prompt you to approve. If your old phone is lost or broken, contact our support team for manual transfer assistance.

VendorPro supports 58mm and 80mm Bluetooth thermal receipt printers that use the ESC/POS standard. Most commonly available portable Bluetooth printers in India are compatible.

Subscription payments are processed securely through Razorpay, a leading payment gateway in India. You can pay using UPI, credit/debit cards, or net banking. Your subscription activates immediately after successful payment.
